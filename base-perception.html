Attention — Where Focus Goes

Balancing, Aligning, Sourcing, Engaging. BASE directs where your awareness goes under load. When cognitive silencing clears the noise, BASE determines what you attend to. The upper arc of each petal—the boundary that shapes the field from above.

The Channel — Where Processing Happens

SPACE is not a framework you apply. It is the channel between attention and emergence—the processing conditions that determine what you can perceive under load. When the bar is heavy enough to silence cognitive noise, this is where tension becomes signal. Curiosity first: exploring or grasping?

Emergence — What Arises

Wisdom, Adaptation, Variance, Exploration, Synergy. WAVES is what emerges when training tension processes through the channel. Not constructed. Not forced. The lower arc of each petal—what surfaces from below when conditions allow.

Integrated Field

Tension processed with attention channeled toward intention. The full tripartite architecture in every rep: BASE shapes the field, SPACE enables transmission, WAVES emerge. Each petal is the complete mechanism—upper arc, void, lower arc. One inseparable dynamic.
