Ages 16–19
Signal Training
15 seizures per month. Constant internal surveillance — tracking metabolic triggers, sleep correlations, pre-ictal signals with five-second warning windows. Reading broke. Brain rerouted: navigation by Pitch, by Salience, by pattern instead of sequence.

Age 19
The Trade-Off Measured
UNC Chapel Hill, 1991. Visual-spatial memory: 99th percentile. Visuospatial skills: 91st. Verbal list memory: 7th. Confrontation naming: below 1st. Shown a funnel — said "flask," "filter," then: "Gosh, I know what that is. I just used one yesterday to put oil in my car."
The gift-cost paradox in clinical data. The seven over the one. Not chosen — carved.
Ages 17–28
Compounding Storms
Meningitis at 17 — ICP at 84, normal below 20. Emergency ventriculostomy. Stopped breathing on day two. Lobectomy at 21 — temporal lobe, left amygdala, hippocampus removed. Last seizure at 28. Spatial intelligence keeps developing through it all.
Two craniotomies. Two hemispheres. The internal navigation system refining under conditions that would break most modeling.
Ages 21–44
The Silent Laboratory
Decades of load-based exploration. The gym as neurological rehabilitation. Progressive resistance revealing neural organization — bodyweight compensations exposed, true patterns emerging under load. Principles practiced daily before they had names. Medication constraining cognition: could practice, couldn't translate.
Balancing. Aligning. Sourcing. Engaging. — unnamed, embodied, running.
Age 44
The Inflection
Anti-seizure medication stopped. Brain fog lifts. Thirty years of spatial modeling suddenly available for linguistic translation. Not new capacity arriving — existing capacity becoming expressible. The bridge from felt sense to teachable framework.
The spatial intelligence was always running. Language arrived last.
37 years of intentional bionetwork exploration. The external spatial intelligence — wheels, cockpits, carrier approaches — redirected inward at sixteen and never stopped developing. What a two-year-old studied in hubcap geometry, a fifty-three-year-old now teaches through fascial signal processing. What necessity carved, methodology now teaches.
