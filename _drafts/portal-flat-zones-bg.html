The Ratio You Were Never Told About

Spatial Strength Training

Your nervous system contains seven interoceptive sensors — sensors for how you are — for every one proprioceptive sensor tracking where you are. Seven to one. That's your biological wiring.

Yet most training obsesses over the one. Position. Form. Numbers. Reps completed. Weight lifted. The entire industry built on 12.5% of your sensory architecture while the other 87.5% runs unattended.

BASEWAVES reverses this. Under sufficient load, your brain shunts fuel from the prefrontal cortex to the motor cortex — a documented neurological event called cognitive silencing. The mental manager that calculates, compares, and catastrophizes goes quiet. Not through discipline. Through metabolic demand.

What remains when the noise drops isn't emptiness. It's signal. Your seven sensors, finally audible.

This is how you develop internal spatial intelligence — conscious access to the vast neural territory that cognitive chaos usually drowns out. It transfers. To eating. To stress. To how you hold uncertainty. The gym is the lab. The capacity is yours to keep.
