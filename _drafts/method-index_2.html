Method

Third rep. The bar settles across your traps and something quiets. Not the weight — that's heavier than last week. The noise. Under this load, your prefrontal cortex can't maintain its grip. It starves. And in that metabolic silence, something else comes online. Your left foot shifts — not because you decided to shift it, but because a signal arrived from somewhere below conscious thought. You didn't think that. You received it. That reception is the capacity this method develops.

The Mechanism
Load → Tension → Signal → Processing → Emergence
Each node responds to attention — expand, pulse, reveal. The chain loops. Load arrives, signal emerges, processing happens, emergence reflects, signal feeds back. Continuous, not episodic.
Competent material
Your body is competent material. Every cell collectively knows what to build, when to stop, how to adapt. Training doesn't tell your body what to do — it communicates goals. The barbell provides a constraint. Your cellular collectives navigate toward coherence within that constraint. You don't build capacity. You remove obstacles to capacity that was already present.

The mechanism may involve bioelectric signaling — cellular collectives communicating through voltage gradients, establishing goal-states at tissue scale. This is the frontier where training science meets developmental biology.
