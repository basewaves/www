II. The Practitioner Gap

Why strength training needs a face

What the current close communicates

Portal Zone 6 currently reads:

“The nervous system reading yours has 37 years of training under load and a neurological history that made internal spatial intelligence a survival necessity — not an intellectual interest.”

One paragraph. It gestures at something critical — that strength training developed capacities far beyond physical performance — but doesn’t deliver the evidence.

What the site teaches vs. what it sells

“Give the framework away. Sell the perception.”

The site gives the framework freely: the 7:1 ratio (Origin/Home), tripartite architecture (Portal Zone 1), transfer evidence (Zone 2), developmental arc (Zone 3), entry tiers (Zone 4), orientation check (Zone 5). The visitor who reaches the close understands what Spatial Strength Training does.

What they don’t yet know: Why this practitioner — and not a program.

The answer is neurological. 37 years of training under load — including 15 seizures/month for 12 years — forced the development of spatial intelligence that most brains never access. 1991 neuropsych testing documented the result: 99th percentile visual/spatial, below 1st percentile verbal. The gift and the cost welded together. That’s not a credential. It’s the reason the methodology exists.

Strength training was the mechanism. Not therapy. Not meditation. Not cognitive work. Heavy load silenced cognitive chaos and forced signal processing through somatic pathways — the same mechanism BASEWAVES now teaches. The practitioner’s history is the proof of concept: strength training bridges neuroscience, interoception, and cognitive processing when the conditions are right.

One paragraph doesn’t communicate this. The visitor needs to understand that they’re not hiring a trainer who learned a system — they’re working with someone whose nervous system built the system through decades of training under extreme neurological conditions.

Decision: Three-Sentence Practitioner Presence in Close Zone

No biographical block between dispatches and pricing. No separate zone. The practitioner presence lands in the existing close zone before the CTA — three sentences, signal-relevant introduction, not bio.

What the three sentences communicate:

1

Earned authority through load — 37 years of training under neurological conditions that made interoceptive intelligence a survival tool. 99th/1st percentile split documented by third-party testing. Not a credential — the reason the methodology exists.

2

The bridge claim made concrete — Strength training developed spatial intelligence, cognitive processing, and signal literacy — not just physical capacity. The practitioner’s history is the primary evidence.

3

State Assessment as diagnostic — The practitioner applies the same spatial intelligence developed over 37 years to read your signal environment. Not an intake form. Not a sales call.

Why not a separate zone: A biographical block between the dispatches (manifesto energy) and pricing (conversion energy) breaks the territorial flow. The practitioner presence works within the close, not before it. The full biographical arc lives in Signals as “The Story” — the close zone gestures, the reading delivers.

Implementation: ~20 minutes. Add three sentences to existing close zone before CTA. Resequence so the poetic close (“Teasing my prisms, with silent rhythms”) follows the practitioner presence — it resolves after the evidence grounds it, not before. Unify CTA language.

CTA Inconsistency — Fix Required

Zone 6: “SCHEDULE A STATE ASSESSMENT”

Footer: “Schedule a State Transition Session”

Unify to “Schedule a State Assessment” everywhere. The assessment is the entry point. State Transition Sessions are what happens after.

IV. What the Vault Knows

Material the site doesn’t yet show

From BIO-BASE.md — Origin Narrative Depth

The full origin story contains material not yet surfaced on the deployed site:

  • Pre-verbal spatial intelligence (ages 2–5): obsession with wheels, circular motion — pattern recognition before language
  • F-14 fighter pilot influence (ages 5–13): cockpit geometry, flight deck operations — external spatial intelligence modeled by father
  • 1991 neuropsych testing: 99th percentile visual/spatial, below 1st percentile verbal naming — the documented trade-off
  • Medication cessation inflection (2016): reduced brain fog enabled translation from spatial coherence to linguistic expression — why the framework became teachable at 44, not at 24

The Portal close could surface the 1991 data point. It’s the most credible single piece of evidence: third-party testing, extreme result, directly explains why this methodology exists. Not a claim — a measurement.

From Origin-Story-Communication-Principles

The site should never:

  • Frame the epilepsy narrative as victimhood (“something that happened TO me”)
  • Frame Greg’s capacity as superiority (“I can do what most trainers can’t”)
  • Declare established methodology with 3 clients (“proven system”)

The site should:

  • Frame as intentional exploration under extreme conditions
  • Frame clients as co-lab (“testing whether this transfers”)
  • Frame scale honestly (“three practitioners, the lab continues”)

From 2025-11-12 Website Revamp Discussion

Shift from “transformative strength training framework” to “spatial intelligence framework.”

This shift is now deployed (Origin’s Frontier zone, Portal’s Zone 0.5 ISI bridge). But the document also flagged:

Missing element: practitioner evidence. The site describes the methodology but not why this practitioner’s neurological history is the primary proof that strength training develops capacities beyond physical performance.

This remains the open gap. Transfer evidence cards show outcomes. The developmental arc shows progression. But the bridge claim — that strength training develops interoceptive intelligence, cognitive processing, and signal literacy — lacks its strongest evidence: the practitioner’s own 37-year training history under extreme neurological conditions.

External Validation the Vault Now Holds

Two convergent validations from outside the framework (Tier 2 — independent confirmation):

Cancer researcher conversation: Confirmed that kinetic scaling / metabolic / movement pattern research exists in fragmented academic silos but has no operationalization container. BASEWAVES sits in the gap between endocrinology, neurology, and exercise science — where no billing code exists. Mina’s zero-testosterone strength gains are evidence that falls between specialties.

Chinese medicine physician: Independently confirmed the whole-system processing approach. Western medicine fragments the body into specialist domains; BASEWAVES integrates through load as the common forcing function. Different tradition, same structural insight.

Neither validates the framework scientifically. Both validate that the gap BASEWAVES occupies is real — recognized by practitioners in adjacent fields who see the same fragmentation from different angles.

From the Business Plan

“Give framework away, sell perception.”

The site currently gives the framework (Portal Zones 1–3) and sells entry tiers (Zone 4). But the “perception” — the spatial intelligence developed through decades of training under load — is undertold. The practitioner’s capacity is what a program can’t replicate. This is the evidence gap.

VII. Architecture Principles

Carry forward
1
Every page is a territory to navigate, not a document to read. Prose lives inside spatial structure as annotation, not body.
2
The website does what the methodology teaches. Hemispheric integration in the communication itself.
3
No PRACTICE page. Contact lives in footer. Practitioner presence lives in Portal close (three sentences, not a bio). Full biographical story lives in Signals (“The Story”). This prevents drift toward a bio page while ensuring the evidence exists — just in the right territory.
4
“Readings” not “essays.” The word holds: reading a signal, reading a territory, reading the body.
5
Filter, don’t convert. The grasping/exploring binary isn’t gatekeeping — it’s the methodology applied to its own audience. Recognition over conversion.
6
Give framework away, sell perception. The site teaches freely. What it sells is the human capacity in the room.
7
The methodology diagnoses itself. “But she’s looking in only one direction” — the frontier’s invisibility is itself a symptom of undeveloped ISI. The site should enact this recursive quality wherever possible: the framework applied to its own audience, its own positioning, its own blind spots. When Origin says most training ignores 87.5% of your sensors, the site must not ignore them either.
