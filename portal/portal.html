Internal Spatial Intelligence

What the ratio means in practice

You're wired 7-to-1 for sensing state over tracking position. Origin told you the ratio. Portal trains it.

Internal spatial intelligence is the capacity to consciously navigate your own biological signaling architecture — the seven interoceptive channels that sense state, process stress, regulate adaptation, and compose response. Not as metaphor. As trainable skill.

Under sufficient load, your brain shunts fuel from the prefrontal cortex to the motor cortex — a metabolic event that researchers call transient hypofrontality. BASEWAVES calls this cognitive silencing — the applied mechanism through which load quiets the mental manager that calculates, compares, and catastrophizes. Not through discipline. Through biology.

What remains when the noise drops isn't emptiness. It's signal. Your seven sensors, finally audible.

It transfers. To metabolic signal literacy — sensing what your body needs, when it needs it, and how training interacts with fuel, sleep, and stress. To how you hold uncertainty when the room changes and your body knows before your mind catches up. The seven sensors don’t just read movement. They read metabolic state. The session is the lab. The capacity is yours to keep.
